# HR Analytics Dashboard 👥📊

A comprehensive **Power BI Dashboard** designed to track, analyze, and visualize key human resources metrics. This project transforms raw HR data into actionable business insights to help organizations understand workforce demographics and optimize employee retention.

## 🌟 Key Features & Insights
* **Executive Summary KPIs:** Real-time visibility into total employee count, active workforce, attrition rate, and demographic averages (age, experience).
* **Attrition Breakdown:** Deep-dive analysis of turnover tracking by department, gender, age bands, and job satisfaction levels.
* **Workforce Demographics:** Clear distributions of employees across different age groups and job roles.
* **Salary & Experience Mapping:** Visually explores how salary slabs and years at the company correlate with attrition trends.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard design and visualization.
* **Power Query:** Data extraction, cleaning, and transformation.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Created custom measures and calculated columns for advanced metrics (e.g., Attrition Rate %).
